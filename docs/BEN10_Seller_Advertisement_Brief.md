# BEN10 — Seller Acquisition Advertisement Brief

## Project

- **Brand:** BEN10 — Omnitrix Marketplace
- **Website:** https://benten-psi.vercel.app
- **Objective:** Acquire qualified gaming sellers who will publish accounts, currency, items, and boosting services on BEN10.
- **Audience:** Game-account traders, currency/top-up sellers, item sellers, and boosting providers who want a premium storefront and trustworthy order flow.
- **Deliverable:** A 20-second, English, 16:9 seller-acquisition advertisement.

## Brand Direction

Use the visible BEN10 website identity as the sole visual reference:

- Near-black backgrounds and panels.
- Lime/Omnitrix green as the principal accent.
- Bold white typography.
- Rounded green action buttons.
- Premium, restrained glass and soft-glow details.
- Seller-marketplace cards and real BEN10 homepage visuals.

Use the BEN10 logo only as a supplied or website-captured composited asset. Do not redraw, reinterpret, morph, or replace it.

## Required Visual Rules

1. Use real BEN10 website screenshots or captured UI areas as composited layers wherever an interface is shown.
2. Do **not** generate fake website pages, fake dashboard recordings, distorted marketplace cards, or unreadable interface text.
3. All on-screen English copy must be crisp, large, correctly spelled composited typography—not AI-generated lettering.
4. Keep each shot focused on one message. Avoid clutter, generic gamer characters, random fantasy scenes, and stock-looking imagery.
5. Publish and feedback flows may be illustrated as BEN10-styled motion graphics. They must not be presented as literal dashboard recordings unless real dashboard footage is supplied.
6. Preserve the BEN10 green-and-black identity throughout; avoid unrelated color palettes.

## Exact 20-Second Storyboard

| Time | Visual direction | Exact on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–3.0s | A single premium offer card powers on from a near-black space. Lime energy traces reveal a real BEN10 marketplace card. Slow cinematic push-in. | **YOUR INVENTORY HAS VALUE.** |
| 3.0–7.0s | The card expands into a clean seller-listing composition. Accounts, currency, items, and boosting icons assemble with precise, premium motion. | **TURN GAME ASSETS INTO SALES.** |
| 7.0–11.0s | A grid of real BEN10 marketplace cards slides into place. A subtle search/discovery motion shows offers reaching buyers. | **REACH REAL BUYERS.** |
| 11.0–15.0s | Three minimal trust indicators animate in sequence: protected checkout, funds held, and live delivery. Use elegant icons, not generic payment imagery. | **SELL WITH CONFIDENCE.** |
| 15.0–18.0s | A verified seller badge and five-star feedback animation rise beside a storefront card. Keep the movement calm, credible, and premium. | **BUILD YOUR REPUTATION.** |
| 18.0–20.0s | End on a clean BEN10 logo lockup over near-black with a subtle lime glow and a final impact. | **SELL WITH BEN10.**<br>**START SELLING TODAY.** |

## Motion and Sound

- Smooth, controlled, premium motion; no chaotic transitions.
- Use cinematic camera pushes, subtle parallax, glass reflections, and gentle green light sweeps.
- Use a modern electronic instrumental track: confident, clean, and energetic without overpowering the copy.
- Add one restrained impact when the final BEN10 lockup appears.

## Quality Requirements

- Export a 1920 × 1080 master in 16:9.
- Use a high-quality final export; avoid low-bitrate compression artifacts, soft details, or blockiness.
- Keep the final video visually sharp on desktop and mobile social-media playback.
- Ensure every headline remains on-screen long enough to be read comfortably.

## Final Review Checklist

- Is the ad clearly aimed at **sellers**, not buyers?
- Are all headlines exactly spelled as provided?
- Does the final frame clearly show BEN10 and “START SELLING TODAY.”?
- Are website visuals authentic and recognisable?
- Are there no AI-generated logos, distorted text, invented product claims, or unrelated characters?
